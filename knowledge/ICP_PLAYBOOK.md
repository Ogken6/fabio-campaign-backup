## ICP 1 — THE SOCIAL COMMERCE SCALER

### Pain Points
- Pipeline chaos — no repeatable sales system
- Founder-led exhaustion — founder closes 80% of deals
- SDRs hired without playbook or ICP
- CRM graveyard with stale leads
- AI implementation gap

### Buying Triggers
- Series A/B raise (0-90 days)
- Job posting for SDR without VP Sales
- Founder posts about GTM/sales challenges
- UK expansion signal
- Monthly MRR growth drops below 8-10%

### Scoring Matrix (60+ = Hot)
| Signal | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Series A/B raise (0-90 days) | 30 |
| SDR job posted, no VP Sales | 25 |
| Founder posts GTM challenges | 20 |
| E-commerce SaaS confirmed | 20 |
| US/Canada HQ + UK expansion | 15 |

| Team size 10-200 | 10 |
| Founder still running demos | 10 |
| Active content last 14 days | 5 |
