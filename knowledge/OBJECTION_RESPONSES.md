## Common Objections

### "I'm not interested"

Response approach:
> I completely understand — this isn't for everyone. Curious though, is it the timing or the problem space?

---

### "We already have a solution"

Response approach:
> That makes sense. What's working about it? What would you improve if you could?

---

### "Send me more information"

Response approach:
> Can do. But before I do — what's the one thing you'd want to know more about? Helps me send what actually matters.

---

### "It's too expensive"

Response approach:
> Interesting — compared to what? The cost of not solving it, or another option you're looking at?

---

### "I don't have time"

Response approach:
> Totally fair. What's the one thing on your plate right now that's taking priority?

---

### "Who else uses this?"

Response approach:
> Happy to share. But first — what specifically would make this relevant to your situation?

---

## Escalation Responses

When a prospect asks about next steps:

> Let's talk. What does your decision-making process look like? No pitch, just understanding.

When a prospect seems ready:

> Should we get on a quick call? I'll send a calendar link, you pick the time that works.

---

## Response Rules

1. Never argue
2. Never push back
3. Ask a genuine question
4. Show interest in their reality
5. Make it easy to say no or yes
6. Give full control to the prospect
