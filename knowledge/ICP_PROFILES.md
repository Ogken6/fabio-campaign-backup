# ICP PROFILES — Ideal Customer Profiles

This file defines who Kenneth targets and how to score them.

---

## ICP 1: Social Commerce Scalers

### Who They Are
Founders who have built a following on social platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) and are now scaling fulfillment.

### Pain Points
- Orders overwhelming DMs and comments
- No system for shipping tracking
- Manual address collection (error-prone)
- Failed deliveries eroding margins
- No inventory management
- Time spent on ops instead of content
- GDPR exposure from storing customer data

### Buying Triggers
- Viral post that crashed their DM system
- Failed delivery batch eating profit
- Customer complaint about shipping
- Hiring first fulfillment staff
- Mention of Shopify/WooCommerce migration
- "Overwhelmed" or "can't keep up" language

### Team Size
5-50 employees

### Funding Stage
Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A or Series B

### Geography
US, Canada

---

## ICP 2: AI Sales Tool Founders

### Who They Are
Founders building AI-powered sales, outreach, or revenue intelligence tools.

### Pain Points
- GTM strategy is an afterthought
- No proven outbound playbook
- Hiring SDRs before having a system
- ICP not defined enough to train reps
- Lead generation not producing qualified pipeline
- Tools not integrating with workflow

### Buying Triggers
- Hiring first sales leader
- Post about SDR challenges
- "Building sales team" language
- Mention of HubSpot/Salesforce migration
- Funding announcement (building engine)
- "GTM" or "sales motion" discussion

### Team Size
10-75 employees

### Funding Stage
Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A or Series B

### Geography
US, Canada
